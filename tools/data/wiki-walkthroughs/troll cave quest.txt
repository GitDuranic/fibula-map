{{Spoiler|name=Troll Cave Quest}}
==Required Equipment==
* [[Rope]]

==Method==
* From the [[Edron]] [[depot]], go down the drain in the south-west corner of the castle ({{Mapper Coords|129.141|124.84|7|3|text=here}}).
* There is only one way to go, so follow this cave down and up until you emerge in a grassy area ({{Mapper Coords|129.105|124.67|7|3|text=here}}).
* Go to the Troll Cave to the north-west ({{Mapper Coords|129.93|124.32|7|3|text=here}}), and go down the hole. Be prepared to face up to 20 [[Troll Champion]]s if no one has been down there for a while. For low level players it's advised to move in slowly, there's a small passage where only one of the [[Troll Champion]]s can attack you.
