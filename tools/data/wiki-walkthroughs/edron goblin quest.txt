{{Spoiler|name=Edron Goblin Quest}}
==Required Equipment==
* [[Rope]]

==Method==
* From the [[Edron]] [[depot]], go down the drain in the south-west corner of the castle ({{Mapper Coords|129.141|124.84|7|3|text=here}}).
* There is only one way to go, so follow the cave until you emerge in a grassy area ({{Mapper Coords|129.105|124.67|7|3|text=here}}).
* Go south-west until you find the goblin hill ({{Mapper Coords|129.83|124.104|7|3|text=here}}). Go down the hole, and be prepared to face up to 10 [[Goblin]]s at once if no one has been down there for a while.
