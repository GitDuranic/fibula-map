{{spoiler|name=Steel Helmet Quest}}

== Required Equipment ==
* [[Shovel]]
* [[Rope]]

== Method ==
''Note: You may also want to do the [[To Outfox a Fox Quest]] at the same time, since it is in the same place.''<br />

Travel west of [[Kazordoon]] past the [[Dwarf Mines]] and use your [[shovel]] to dig a [[Loose Stone Pile]] hidden behind a [[Plum Tree]] located {{Mapper Coords|126.233|124.221|7|4|text=Here}}. You may encounter [[Snake]]s, [[Poison Spider]]s, [[Wolf|Wolves]], [[Bear]]s, [[Bug]]s and [[Dwarf|Dwarves]]. Once you go down the hole, walk west and rope up into the [[Secret Hideout]] {{Mapper Coords|text=here|126.210|124.222|8|4|1|0.250.25}}

Creatures on each of the floors:<br />
'''ground floor''': 2 [[Minotaur Guard]]s, 2 [[Minotaur Archer]]s<br />
'''first floor''': 4 [[Minotaur Guard]]s, 4 [[Minotaur Archer]]s<br />
'''floor 2''': 2 [[Minotaur Guard]]s in each room (north and south rooms)<br />
'''floor 3''': North side: 2 [[Minotaur Guard]]s, 4 [[Minotaur Archer]]s<br />
South side: 2 [[Minotaur Mage]]s, 4 [[Minotaur Archer]]s<br />
'''floor 4''': North side:4 [[Minotaur Archer]]s<br />
South side: 1 [[Minotaur Mage]], 3 [[Minotaur Archer]]s

The rewards are found in different locations across the hideout.
*56 [[Gold Coin]]s can be found in a [[Chest of Drawers (Quest)|Chest of Drawers]] next to a table {{Mapper Coords|text=here|126.204|124.207|5|8|1|0.1250.125}}
*[[Steel Helmet]] can be found in a [[Box (Quest)|Box]] next an anvil {{Mapper Coords|text=here|126.205|124.203|4|8|1|0.1250.125}}
*47 Gold Coins can be found in a [[Chest (Quest)|Chest]] between two beds {{Mapper Coords|text=here|126.208|124.213|5|8|1|0.1250.125}}
*[[The Horned Fox]]'s [[The Fox Is Out (Book)|scroll]] can be found in a [[Chest (Quest)|Chest]] between a bed and a table {{Mapper Coords|text=here|126.211|124.218|4|8|1|0.1250.125}}
