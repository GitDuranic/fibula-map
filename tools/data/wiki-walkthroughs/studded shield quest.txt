{{Spoiler| name=Studded Shield Quest}}
==Required equipment==
None, a [[Shields|shield]] is recommended.
'''Note:''' To take the one on the premium side, 3 [[box]]es are needed.

==Method==
*Head to the [[Bananapalm]] located in {{Mapper Coords|125.172|125.169|7|3|text=East}} [[Rookgaard]], or north-west premium Rookgaard.
*Alternatively, go to the premium side and up the wolf hill, {{Mapper Coords|124.244|125.199|7|4|text=here}}. Take the 3 boxes to the flat part and jump up to the [[Bananapalm]] there.
*"Use" the [[Bananapalm]] to get the [[Banana]].
*Head to [[Willie]] in the town ({{Mapper Coords|125.67|125.205|7|3|text=here}}) and trade the '[[Banana]]' for a [[Studded Shield]].
