{{Spoiler | name=Studded Legs Quest}}
==Required equipment==
*[[Rope]]
*Optional [[Small Health Potion]]s and [[Food]]

== Method ==
*Go to the [[Wasp]] tower in North-west [[Rookgaard]] ({{Mapper Coords|124.255|125.140|7|3|text=here}}).
*Rope yourself up killing or simply ignoring the [[Wasp]]s you will face.
*At the top simply "use" on the [[Honeyflower Patch]] to the right and you will get a [[Honey Flower]].
*Head to [[Lee'Delle]]'s Shop ({{Mapper Coords|125.24|125.196|7|3|text=here}}), on '''premium account area''', and trade your [[Honey Flower]] for a pair of [[Studded Legs]].
**'''Note:''' If you find more [[Honey Flower]]s you can trade those too, there is not a once only limit.
